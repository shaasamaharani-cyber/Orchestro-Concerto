ABOUT THIS PERFORMANCE

A Studio Ghibli Musical Evening

Experience the beloved music of Studio Ghibli brought to life through a breathtaking orchestral performance. Featuring iconic themes from Howl's Moving Castle, Spirited Away, and more, this concert invites you into a world of magic, emotion, and wonder.

From sweeping melodies to deeply emotional compositions, each piece captures the essence of Ghibli's timeless storytelling. The evening reaches its magical finale with Merry-Go-Round of Life, leaving a lasting impression of elegance, nostalgia, and enchantment.
